# State machines — approved and proposed tables

Approval is tracked per entity: the vehicle status vocabulary and safety/derivation principles are owner-approved in D-016; detailed vehicle transitions and most other entity transitions remain proposed. D-021/D-022/D-023/D-030 approve rental-request Draft entry and Draft → Submitted, including an explicit return date for overnight return-time requests. D-029 defines reservation triggers but does not approve the Confirmed/Assigned transitions themselves. D-031 defines fail-closed buffer and release effects but approves no status transitions. D-033 clarifies that the buffer follows the earlier trip and exact boundary contact is allowed, without activating enforcement. D-032 approves only Submitted → Under Review and Under Review → Declined, both for Ops Managers/Super Administrators with a reason. `*` means any nonterminal state. Any implemented change must be service-authorized, enforce preconditions, and preserve status history/audit evidence. Reservation-derived vehicle statuses and assignment lifecycle transitions are not implemented. D-045 adds a separate persisted safety-block ledger, not manual Vehicle status editing.

**D-053 implemented trip lifecycle:** every trip enters at Planned; Planned → Ready for Dispatch/Cancelled, Ready for Dispatch → Dispatched/Cancelled/Planned, Dispatched → In Progress/Delayed/Interrupted, In Progress → Delayed/Interrupted/Completed, Delayed → In Progress/Completed/Interrupted, Interrupted → In Progress/Completed/Cancelled are enforced in `trip-transitions.ts`, the service (row lock, permission, dispatch-resource precondition, history + audit), and database guards, with a full 8×8 matrix unit test.

**D-052 implemented booking lifecycle:** staff/HR bookings enter directly at Pending (Draft is reserved for the future passenger portal); Pending → Accepted/Rejected/Cancelled, Accepted → Assigned/Cancelled, Assigned → Boarded/Cancelled/Accepted, and Boarded → Completed are enforced in `booking-transitions.ts`, the service (row lock, permission, history + audit), and database guards. This slice exposes only accept/reject/cancel to staff; assignment (BKG-04), boarding/completion (DRV-02) and cancellation deadlines/waiting list (BKG-05) arrive in their slices.

**D-049 implemented contract lifecycle:** the rows above plus Pending Approval → Draft are implemented in `contract-transitions.ts`, enforced in the service layer (row lock, permission, preconditions) and in the database (status/transition checks, transition guard, append-only history triggers). Approval requires an active same-company client and start/end dates with end on or after start; Suspended reactivation is refused past the end date; Expired renewal requires a new end date that has not passed. Terminated is terminal. No automatic expiry job exists; expiry is a manual reason-recorded transition, and Active contracts past their end date surface warnings.

**D-061/D-062 implemented corporate invoice lifecycle (Phase 1C):** `DRAFT → ISSUED → PAID`, `VOID` from `DRAFT`/`ISSUED`; `PAID`/`VOID` terminal, implemented in `invoice-transitions.ts`, enforced in the service layer (invoice row lock, `invoice:manage`/`payment:record` company-or-global scope, preconditions) and in the database (status/transition checks, change guard, deferred history pairing, append-only history/payment triggers). There is no Pending Approval status and no stored Partially Paid status: part-payment is represented by append-only `InvoicePayment` rows and the outstanding balance is always derived (`total − Σ payments`); an invoice becomes PAID automatically when payments reach the total, or manually from ISSUED with a reason. Voiding is refused once any payment exists, and voiding releases the invoice's trips and its contract+period (trigger-maintained `billingActive` flag) so a replacement invoice can be generated. No Overdue status or scheduled process exists.

**D-016 approved vehicle boundary:** use only the seven vehicle status names in the standing brief. Do not add `Retired`. New vehicle records receive no status default, and the current vehicle register must not expose manual Vehicle status editing. Reserved/Assigned/On Trip will derive from authoritative related records. D-045 records safety blocks separately and exposes active safety-block state in the fleet register; assignment prevention is not yet implemented.

**D-018 approved conflict/release policy:** apply a safety block immediately and prevent new assignments. Flag future assignments for dispatcher resolution without auto-cancelling or reassigning them; use the incident/replacement workflow for a vehicle already on a trip. A Fleet Manager/authorized releaser may return a vehicle to Available after work completion and an inspection record; a separate inspector role is not required. D-019 records who may apply safety blocks. D-045 implements immediate block application and recorded passed-inspection release. Assignment conflict resolution and active-trip incident/replacement workflows remain unimplemented; assignment prevention cannot yet be demonstrated because assignments do not exist.

**D-021/D-022/D-023/D-030 approved rental-request boundary:** staff enquiries are initially saved as Draft; submission is separate. Drafts may omit both return time and duration; before submission at least one is required, with duration measured in positive whole minutes. A request links to an existing customer. If return time is earlier than departure, an explicit return date after service date is required before submission; never infer the next day. D-023/D-030 approve only Draft → Submitted, its required fields, and Ops Manager/Super Administrator authority. D-032 additionally approves only Submitted → Under Review → Declined for Ops Managers/Super Administrators, with required reason/history/audit. No quotation or resource work is implied. D-029 defines future reservation triggers but does not approve the Confirmed/Assigned transitions. D-031 requires explicit company-configured vehicle/driver buffers and prevents release by scheduled time alone; D-033 applies the appropriate buffer after the earlier-ending trip; release effects require actual authorized cancellation/completion/return/assignment-end transitions, none of which are implemented or approved here. Except for the D-032 review/decline subset, every later transition in the proposed table remains unapproved and unimplemented.

Actor names are roles, not a substitute for resource/action permission checks. The D-046 maintenance work-order graph is the implemented, bounded exception; other proposed matrices and ambiguous transitions remain unapproved. D-046 terminal work-order states cannot be changed.
