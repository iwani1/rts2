## Direction carried forward
The standing brief's own visual constraints remain authoritative: navy primary, white/neutral backgrounds, subtle teal, clear typography, readable tables and status indicators. Apply restrained, compact structure to admin operations, with role-specific navigation/dashboards and a materially different mobile-first driver experience. Keep decorative effects out of high-frequency operational paths. Define spacing, type, tokens, focus, responsive behavior and motion preferences before the first feature UI. No proprietary branding, stock imagery, guessed contact details or placeholder operational data.

## Remaining design research before UI work
The supplied sources are reviewed to accessible extent above, but this does not replace inspection of actual authenticated app flows in Mobbin (gated) or implementation/source examples behind Scrolltide's paid library. When UI phase is approved, select concrete app examples accessible without implying gated review; record desktop/mobile behavior and accessibility behavior for the relevant pages/components before coding.
